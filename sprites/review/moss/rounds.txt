Moss: three problems per round, then fixed. Images are numbered in order.

Before (00): reads as a sheep or a grey rat with a disc on its back. Shell is a front-on circle, head grey and muzzle-like, legs human.

Portrait
 Round 1 (01): head and neck one tall column (sock puppet); skin split into flat sand/rose bands; plates look like bricks, spanner a stick.
 Round 2 (02): face cut into horizontal bands with a brown "beard" jaw; kelp-green far rim reads as another material; brass patch a flat box.
 Round 3 (03-04): rivets and sheen made an X, then four dark pips (a die); rose band across the forehead; flat heavy brow looked stern.
 Round 4 (05, 06): pleased barely differed from neutral; annoyed too close to confused; proud's glint invisible.
 After (20): smiling crescent eyes for pleased, dark steep brows for annoyed, a visible glint for proud; six bright rivets along the patch edges.

World sprite
 Round 1 (10): small head with a blob eye on a stick neck; rim band eaten by the outline; far leg looked like a brown boot, tail floating.
 Round 2 (11): rim shadow 4-5 px wide made a dark wedge; arm read as part of the neck; shell lower half flat.
 Round 3 (12-13): apron and far leg sank into brown floorboards; arm still merged with the neck; walk swing barely visible.
 After (21, 23, 24): rust apron and skin-toned far leg stand off the floor; arm set back with a shoulder shadow; silhouette is a dome shell, beaked head and stubby legs at 1x.
