# The cast: how they look

These characters are based on real people. Check that each person is happy to appear in a paid product, and let them see their character before launch.

This file holds written descriptions only. **Never add photos of anyone to this repository** (it may become public). The owner sends reference photos to the illustrator directly. If the owner changes a description, update this file, `art/ARTIST_BRIEF.md` and, if an image changes, `art/manifest.json` (run `npx tsx scripts/build-art-manifest.ts`).

`art/pixel-reference/` has rough pixel portraits drawn from these descriptions (`cast-sheet.png` shows everyone side by side). They're a quick guide to colours and hair only, not the style, and they don't match the real faces. Where a reference and this file differ, this file wins.

## Everyone

All six are young adults, late teens to about twenty, drawn as they are: friendly and natural, not idealised into models and not caricatured. Keep proportions true to the style in the artist brief (about 6.5 to 7 heads tall). Never exaggerate or comment on body shape. Nobody is the butt of the joke.

Each has an everyday outfit (below) and, for the group illustration only, an optional matching school-uniform outfit: a pale grey-blue short-sleeved shirt.

## Paul, the main professor

Signature colour: sun yellow `#FFB627`.

- **Cap and hair:** a **black cap worn backwards**. Only a little wavy dark-brown hair shows under it, **at the back and just behind the ears**; it doesn't hang down. The backwards cap is his signature look.
- **Face:** strong dark eyebrows, brown eyes, light beard stubble and a thin moustache. A big, open grin when he's pleased, an easy half-smile otherwise.
- **Skin:** light, warm olive.
- **Glasses:** no.
- **Everyday outfit:** a navy-and-cream varsity jacket (navy body, cream sleeves) over a collared shirt, or a white polo shirt with black trousers. A black smartwatch.
- **Distinctive detail:** the backwards black cap, with a little wavy hair showing at the back. In the "thinking" expression he pushes the cap back or rubs the back of his neck. In the final scene (the Clocktower's clock running again) he wears a small gold paper crown, on top of the cap or in place of it (the artist's choice).
- **Pixel reference:** `art/pixel-reference/paul.png`.

## Neil, the sub professor

Signature colour: teal `#1FB5A8`.

- **Hair:** short black hair, a little longer and swept up at the front, shorter at the sides.
- **Face:** thick dark eyebrows, brown eyes, clean-shaven, a wide, friendly smile.
- **Skin:** light-medium, warm.
- **Glasses:** yes, **black, slightly rectangular frames**. His signature.
- **Everyday outfit:** a white collared shirt, sleeves sometimes rolled up.
- **Distinctive detail:** the black rectangular glasses, which he pushes up his nose when he's debugging (his "thinking" expression).

## Aaesha, classmate

Signature colour: coral `#FF7A6B`.

- **Hair:** very long, straight, dark-brown to near-black hair with a centre parting, falling well past the shoulders, often with a few strands framing her face.
- **Face:** defined dark eyebrows, dark-brown eyes, rosy cheeks, a warm closed-mouth smile.
- **Skin:** light, warm.
- **Glasses:** no.
- **Jewellery:** small gold earrings, a fine gold necklace, a gold bangle.
- **Everyday outfit:** a fitted dark-brown T-shirt and blue denim.
- **Distinctive detail:** the gold jewellery and the warm smile.

## Caitlin, classmate

Signature colour: violet `#8C6BF2`.

- **Hair:** long, dark-brown hair **turning purple towards the ends** (from about chin height), centre parting, straight with a soft wave, past the shoulders.
- **Face:** dark-brown eyes, a calm, slightly knowing closed-mouth smile.
- **Skin:** medium-brown, warm.
- **Glasses:** yes, **round, thin black frames**. Her signature.
- **Everyday outfit:** relaxed and casual: a loose overshirt over a plain T-shirt, unless the owner says otherwise.
- **Distinctive detail:** the round glasses and the two-tone, purple-ended hair.

## Riya, classmate

Signature colour: leaf green `#4CC36F`.

- **Hair:** long, straight, glossy jet-black hair with a sharp centre parting, **falling behind her shoulders**.
- **Face:** strong, well-defined dark eyebrows, dark-brown eyes, a playful closed-mouth smile.
- **Skin:** light-medium, warm.
- **Glasses:** no.
- **Everyday outfit:** a cream varsity jacket with navy stripes on the collar and cuffs, over a dark top. Pastel lilac nails.
- **Distinctive detail:** the varsity jacket, and a finger heart in her "pleased" expression.

## Yahya, classmate

Signature colour: blue `#3B6FD8`.

- **Hair:** dark, almost black, short tight curls **piled on top, with a few falling onto his forehead**, and shorter sides.
- **Face:** dark eyebrows, dark-brown eyes, **clean-shaven** (no stubble, no moustache). A cool, unbothered look by default. His smile is rare and earned, which suits his competitive streak.
- **Skin:** light-medium, warm olive.
- **Glasses:** no.
- **Everyday outfit:** a black jacket over a heather-grey T-shirt.
- **Distinctive detail:** the curly top and the deadpan expression. In his "proud" expression he throws a peace sign.

## The duck

A yellow rubber duck with a chipped beak. It lives on Neil's desk, has no name and never speaks.
