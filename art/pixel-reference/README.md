# Pixel references of the cast

Quick pixel portraits of Paul, Neil, Aaesha, Caitlin, Riya and Yahya, so the whole cast can be seen at a glance. `cast-sheet.png` has everyone side by side with their role and look; each person also has their own file (`paul.png` and so on).

They're drawn from the written appearance sheet and the owner's choices. No photos were used, so details such as exact face shape won't match the real people. The owner's picks (October 2026):

- Paul: a black cap worn backwards, with just a little wavy hair showing underneath at the back and sides (option D)
- Neil: short, swept up at the front, short sides (option A), black rectangular glasses
- Aaesha: as first drawn
- Caitlin: dark brown hair turning purple from about chin height (option A), round black glasses
- Riya: long straight hair behind her shoulders (option B)
- Yahya: tight curls on top with a few falling onto the forehead (option B), clean-shaven

`options-sheet.png` and `options/` keep the alternatives that were offered.

They're a reference, not the course's art: the app doesn't use them. The real characters will be hand-drawn by an illustrator from `art/ARTIST_BRIEF.md`. Until then, the app shows each character as a circle in their colour with their initial.

To redraw after changing a description or a pick (the picks are `DEFAULTS` at the top of the script):

```
node scripts/draw-cast-pixels.mjs
```
