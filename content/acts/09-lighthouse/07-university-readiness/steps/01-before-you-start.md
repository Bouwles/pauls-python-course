Six questions, each a function, each in the list of steps. Take them in any order.

1. **Read every question first.** Two minutes. While your hands do the easy ones, your head is already chewing on the hard ones.
2. **Start with the one you can do.** One finished answer beats three started ones, and it settles the nerves.
3. **Write a test before the code.** Each starter ends with the question's examples as `assert` lines. Add the edge cases before you write the function: empty, one item, duplicates, the biggest input allowed. Watch them fail.
4. **Read the limits.** "Up to 200,000 prices" is a hint about how fast it must be. A loop inside a loop over 200,000 items is forty billion steps.
5. **Leave fifteen minutes to check.** Reread each question against your answer. Most marks are lost to a misread question, not a bug.
