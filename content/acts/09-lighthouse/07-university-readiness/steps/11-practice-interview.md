  - paul.neutral.point: "Two minutes, out loud. Writing it down doesn't count."
test_plan: false
evidence: none
checklist:
  - id: one-sentence
    text: "It says what the project is in one sentence, before any detail."
    why: "If they can't repeat it back after one sentence, the next twenty won't help."
  - id: who-for
    text: "It names who the project is for, and what they did before it existed."
    why: "The class trip fund lived in a notebook and caused an argument a week. That's why the tracker exists. Say the why."
  - id: one-decision
    text: "It explains one decision, and what that decision cost."
    why: "Every choice has a price. Naming it shows you saw it, rather than stumbled into it."
  - id: a-limitation
    text: "It admits one thing the project doesn't do, or does badly."
    why: "They'll find it anyway. Better they hear it from you, along with what you'd do about it."
  - id: a-number
    text: "It quotes at least one real number: rows, seconds, tests, users."
    why: "Fast is an opinion. Under a second for six thousand bookings is a measurement."
  - id: how-you-know
    text: "It says how you know it works: the tests, and one bug they caught."
    why: "It works is a claim. A test that caught the finish learning from itself is evidence."
  - id: plain-words
    text: "It uses plain words, and explains any term the listener might not know."
    why: "Jargon hides whether you understand. Plain words prove it."
  - id: stops
    text: "It stops, and waits for the next question."
    why: "The last sentence is usually the weakest. End on the number, then be quiet."
---
Now say it out loud. Choose your capstone, or the Job you're proudest of. Set a timer for two minutes and explain it to the duck, to someone patient, or to your phone's recorder.

Then listen back, or think back, and go through Paul's list. He won't hear it, so the ticks are on your honour: tick only what you actually did.

Do it twice. The second time is always better, and that's the point. Interviews reward the people who have said it out loud before.
