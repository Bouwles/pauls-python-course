A game isn't finished until strangers have played it. Find three people, ideally ones who haven't watched you build it, sit them in front of it, and say nothing. Write down where each one hesitates, gets stuck, or laughs.

Then change something because of what you saw: the speed at level 1, how often boots fall, how clear game over is. Put the change under a test, so the next change can't undo it by accident.

Last, the handover. Rewrite the README for Caitlin: how to install it, start it, play it, and clear the high scores. Hand in the pytest report, and paste a link to the repository.
