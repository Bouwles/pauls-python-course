The write-up is for someone who has read the paper and is deciding whether to trust your reproduction.

Open with what you reproduced, at what scale, and how closely. Then the main figure, and how you made it. Then your own experiment, with its prediction and what actually happened.

Then the section that matters most: what didn't match. The paper's experiments were bigger, with real data and different features; yours fits on a laptop. Where your curve differs from theirs, say how, and give your best guess why, marked as a guess. A mismatch you explain honestly is worth more than a match you can't.

Cite the paper in full, and say how to rerun everything.
