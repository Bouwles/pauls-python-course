1. Write your question in one sentence that could come back yes or no. "Is Stornoway getting wetter?" is a start. "Were 1991 to 2020 wetter than 1891 to 1920, by more than two thirty-year spells usually differ?" can be answered.
2. Name the columns and the years it needs, and check the file has them.
3. Write down which years you'll compare, and why, before drawing a single chart.
4. Note which marks could matter: estimates, the sensor, provisional months.
5. Write your test plan: at least three checks your answer stands on, each with what you expect. "Every year I compare has all twelve months of rain."
6. Write one thing this data can't tell anyone, whatever the answer.
