Two sides of paper, charts included, not a thesis.

1. First paragraph: the answer. What changed, by how much, and how sure you are.
2. Then how you got there, with the charts.
3. Then what this can't tell anyone: one station, a sensor that changed in 2002, the gaps, the months still provisional.
4. Then the credit line from `SOURCE.md`, and how to rerun it.

Yahya will check every number, so each must be printed by `investigation.py` or readable off a chart. Watch for words that claim more than you showed: "proves", "because" and "always" rarely survive a careful reader; "suggests" and "at this station" usually do.
