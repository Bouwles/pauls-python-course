Charts first, then words.

1. Draw the charts your question needs, each titled with the part it answers, axes labelled with units, starting at zero or saying so.
2. Show the year-to-year wobble before claiming a change: a dot for each year, with a ten-year running average over the dots. Charts to be compared share scales.
3. Try one other reasonable choice: other periods, the median, or the provisional months kept in. If the answer changes, that goes in the write-up.
4. Put `investigation.py`, `station.py`, your tests and the charts in a repository or a gist, and paste the link.
