The fleet's log is in `fishing.csv`: every day from 2023 to 2025. Each row has what was known at dawn (wind speed and direction, pressure, sea temperature, cloud, rain, swell, the tide's range and the first high water) and what happened after: how many boats went out, and how many boxes each landed. Ferro calls a day good at eight boxes a boat.

Three rules hold for the whole job:

1. **Only what's known at dawn.** The forecast is made before the boats leave.
2. **2025 is in the drawer.** Weather comes in spells, so a shuffled split would test each day beside its twin from the same week. Learn from 2023 and 2024; open 2025 once, at the end.
3. **Beat the baselines.** Ferro's own rule, a tide range of 4 m or more and under 20 knots, and always saying no.
