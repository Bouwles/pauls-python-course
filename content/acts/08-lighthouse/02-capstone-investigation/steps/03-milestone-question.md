One question, in one sentence. "Is Stornoway getting wetter?" is a start. "Were 1991 to 2020 wetter than 1891 to 1920, by more than two thirty-year spells usually differ?" can actually be answered.

Write down now which years you'll compare, and why. Picking them after seeing the chart is how anyone proves anything.

Then the test plan: the checks your answer depends on, each with what you expect to see. "Every year I compare has all twelve months of rain." "No provisional month falls in my years, or I say which do." The Make checked the loading; these checks are about your question.
