The last part of this job is your own data story: a question, a public dataset that can answer it, the same four pieces, and a web page.

Choosing the data:

- **A question first.** "Has the river got higher?" beats "something about rivers".
- **Who made it, and how.** A national statistics office, a met service, a university repository. Read what they say about how it was collected, and what's missing.
- **The licence.** Open Government Licence, CC BY, CC0 or public domain mean you can publish your work, saying where the data came from. No licence means ask first.
- **Small enough to understand.** A few thousand rows you've read beats a million you haven't.

Then publish the page anywhere that serves a web page for free: GitHub Pages, from your repository, is one.
