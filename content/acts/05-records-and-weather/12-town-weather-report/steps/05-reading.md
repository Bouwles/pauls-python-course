Aaesha's notes:

1. The bars start at 10.8, so the 2020s stand nineteen times taller than the 1960s. Draw from zero, or draw the difference from an average and say which.
2. No units, and no source. Degrees C, from what, cleaned how?
3. "11% warmer" means nothing. Zero degrees isn't no temperature, so a temperature can't be a percentage of another. Say degrees.
4. "Down 35 a year" with p of 0.21: shuffled years do that one time in five. That isn't a finding. Say the record shows no clear change.
5. "The sun is coming out": p of 0.43. The same.
6. One station can show that one place has warmed. It can't say why. Claim what the data shows, then stop.

Yahya's numbers were all right, and the draft still said four things that aren't true. Keep these six in mind when you write your own conclusion.
