A **program** is a list of instructions for a computer, written in a language it can follow. This course uses Python.

Each line is one instruction. When you press **Run**, Python carries out the lines one at a time, starting at the top and working down. Anything the program shows appears in the **Output** panel.

The program on the right has three lines, and each one shows a few words. Press Run: the words appear in the same order as the lines.

People often expect the computer to work out what they meant, or to pick its own order. It never does. It does exactly what each line says, from the top down.
